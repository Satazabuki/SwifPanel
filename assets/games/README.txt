Game banners for the home page and the order screens
====================================================

Put one picture per game in this folder, named after the game:

    cs16.jpg      Counter-Strike 1.6          rust.jpg       Rust
    cs2.jpg       Counter-Strike 2            valheim.jpg    Valheim
    minecraft.jpg Minecraft                   zomboid.jpg    Project Zomboid
    gmod.jpg      Garry's Mod                 7dtd.jpg       7 Days to Die
    tf2.jpg       Team Fortress 2             palworld.jpg   Palworld
    l4d2.jpg      Left 4 Dead 2

The picture is used as the background of the game's card (shown at 16:9 and cropped to fill, so keep the
important part in the middle) and, cropped square, for the small icons. Best: 16:9, at least 640x360 pixels.
Accepted: .jpg, .webp, .png, .svg. A dark fade is added at the bottom automatically so the name and price stay
readable on any picture.

A game without a file shows a coloured banner with a large pictogram, so nothing looks broken.

Use official key art or logos only where the publisher allows it: publishers provide press kits and brand
guidelines, and Steam shows each game's artwork on its store page. Get permission or follow their rules.
